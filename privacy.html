Privacy policy

Applies to the ST Module Engine extension for SillyTavern (the "extension").

Summary

The extension has no server and no user accounts of its own. The author of the extension does not receive, store or have access to any of your data.

What the extension accesses

If you choose to turn on optional cloud synchronization and sign in with Google, the extension asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With it the extension can only see and change the files and the folder it created itself in your Google Drive (a folder named "ST Module Engine Sync"). It cannot see any other file in your Drive, and it does not read your name, e-mail address or profile.

Those files contain the SillyTavern data you selected to synchronize (for example characters, chats, lorebooks and backgrounds), so that your own devices can keep them in step.

Where data goes

  • Data goes only between your browser and your own Google Drive (or Dropbox) account. There is no intermediate server operated by the author.
  • The sign-in tokens issued by Google are stored in your own SillyTavern settings on your computer. They are used only to talk to your Drive.
  • Nothing is shared with, sold to or used by third parties, and nothing is used for advertising or to train any model.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can disconnect the account at any time in the extension (the tokens are deleted from your settings), and you can revoke the extension's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the "ST Module Engine Sync" folder from your Drive at any time.

Contact

Questions can be asked in the issue tracker at github.com/IAmiGOI/Module-Engine.
